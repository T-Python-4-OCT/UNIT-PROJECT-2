From dirt pitches to world stages

For a long time, fun in the Kingdom meant football on dirt pitches, trips into the desert and evenings with family. Cinemas closed in the early 1980s and stayed shut for 35 years.

Football carried the country's biggest moments. Saudi Arabia won the Asian Cup in 1984, and on its World Cup debut in 1994 it beat Belgium with Saeed Al-Owairan's famous goal to reach the round of 16.

Vision 2030 changed the picture. The General Entertainment Authority was founded in 2016. In 2018 women entered football stadiums and cinemas reopened. Today Riyadh Season, Formula 1 in Jeddah and the Esports World Cup bring the world to the Kingdom.
