One country, two different worlds

In 1932, King Abdulaziz united the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Back then, most people lived in mud houses or as Bedouin in the desert, travelling by camel and living from farming, trade and pearl diving.

The discovery of oil in 1938 changed everything. And since 2016, Vision 2030 has opened a new chapter: new cities, new industries and a country open to the world.
