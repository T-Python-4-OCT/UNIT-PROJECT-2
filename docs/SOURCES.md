## Project facts

| Project | Facts and milestones | Primary sources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Collection | PIF groups these developments under five giga-project entities. The Red Sea and AMAALA are distinct destinations developed by Red Sea Global, one of those five entities. | [PIF giga-project portfolio](https://www.pif.gov.sa/en/our-investments/giga-projects/) |
| NEOM | Northwest location, industry and logistics, Trojena plans and the operating airport and port. Airport operations began in 2019. Oxagon was announced on 16 November 2021. The port transferred to NEOM in 2022. | [NEOM overview and timeline](https://www.neom.com/en-us/about), [NEOM Oxagon announcement](https://www.neom.com/en-us/newsroom/hrh-prince-announces-oxagon) |
| Qiddiya | City development near Riyadh, with Six Flags, Aquarabia and PlayMaker Studios. Qiddiya Investment Company launched in 2018. Six Flags opened on 31 December 2025 and Aquarabia opened to the public on 23 April 2026. | [Qiddiya City](https://qiddiya.com/projects/qiddiya-city/), [PIF giga-project portfolio](https://www.pif.gov.sa/en/our-investments/giga-projects/), [Six Flags lead design consultant](https://www.atkinsrealis.com/en/projects/qiddiya), [Qiddiya Aquarabia opening announcement](https://qiddiya.com/media-centre/media-centre-listing/aquarabia-qiddiya-city-officially-welcomes-visitors-/) |
| Diriyah | At-Turaif anchors the development and its Najdi design. UNESCO inscribed At-Turaif in 2010. The development authority formed in 2017. Bujairi Terrace and At-Turaif opened to visitors as project assets in December 2022. | [Diriyah Company overview](https://www.diriyahcompany.sa/en), [UNESCO World Heritage listing](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1329), [Diriyah Company and the authority](https://www.diriyahcompany.sa/en/about), [Diriyah Company first openings](https://www.diriyahcompany.sa/en/news/diriyah-showcases-its-offering-to-the-world-at-the-arabian-travel-market) |
| ROSHN | Residential projects across multiple cities, including SEDRA, ALAROUS and ALDANAH. PIF launched ROSHN on 19 August 2020. ALAROUS was announced on 24 May 2022. Sales of SEDRA's fourth phase began on 4 March 2024. | [ROSHN communities](https://www.roshn.sa/), [PIF SEDRA overview](https://www.pif.gov.sa/en/our-investments/giga-projects/roshn/sedra/), [PIF ALAROUS overview](https://www.pif.gov.sa/en/our-investments/giga-projects/roshn/alarous/), [PIF ROSHN launch](https://www.pif.gov.sa/en/news-and-insights/press-releases/2020/roshn/), [PIF ALAROUS announcement](https://www.pif.gov.sa/ar/news-and-insights/newswire/2022/pif-owned-real-estate-developer-roshn-announces-al-arous-community-in-jeddah/), [PIF 2024 offering circular (PDF)](https://www.pif.gov.sa/-/media/project/pif-corporate/pif-corporate-site/our-financials/capital-markets-program/pdf/international-bond-offering-circular-2024.pdf) |
| The Red Sea | West coast resorts and coral and mangrove restoration. The first resort received guests in 2023. Shebara and Desert Rock opened in 2024. Shura Island opened with initial resorts in 2025. | [Red Sea Global destination overview and timeline](https://www.redseaglobal.com/en/portfolio/the-red-sea/) |
| AMAALA | Triple Bay, the Corallium Marine Life Institute and wellness resorts. The project was announced in 2018. Red Sea Global announced the opening of Triple Bay on 11 November 2025 and reported Jayasom's opening on 16 September 2026. | [Red Sea Global destination overview and timeline](https://www.redseaglobal.com/en/portfolio/amaala/), [Triple Bay opening announcement](https://www.redseaglobal.com/en/media-center/news/red-sea-global-announces-the-opening-of-amaala-the-world-s-new-coastal-lifestyle-destination/), [Jayasom opening announcement](https://www.redseaglobal.com/en/media-center/news/Red-Sea-Global-announces-the-opening-of-Jayasom-Wellness-Resort-advancing-AMAALAs-wellness-vision/) |
