Ruya means "vision" in Arabic. This site tells the story of Saudi Vision 2030 through its giga-projects.

Saudi Vision 2030

A Plan for the Kingdom's Future

Saudi Vision 2030 is the Kingdom's long-term plan to diversify its economy, develop public services and open the country to the world. It was launched on 25 April 2016 by HRH Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman.

The Vision is built on three pillars and 96 strategic objectives, delivered through Vision Realization Programs and large-scale giga-projects like the ones featured on this site.

Three Pillars

What the Vision Stands On

01 diversity_3

A Vibrant Society

A society proud of its identity, with a better quality of life, healthcare and culture.

Example goal Raise average life expectancy to 80 years
02 trending_up

A Thriving Economy

A diverse, competitive economy that supports investment, entrepreneurs and new jobs.

Example goal Grow the private sector's share of GDP from 40% to 65%
03 flag

An Ambitious Nation

An effective, transparent government and citizens who take responsibility.

Example goal Raise non-oil government revenue to SAR 1 trillion a year
