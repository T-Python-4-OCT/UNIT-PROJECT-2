The Vision

An Ambitious Nation,
A Thriving Future

Launched in 2016, Saudi Vision 2030 is a national plan to diversify the economy, empower society and open the Kingdom to the world. At the heart of this transformation are the giga-projects: large-scale developments that celebrate Saudi heritage, create new destinations for tourism and entertainment, and raise the quality of life for citizens and visitors alike.
