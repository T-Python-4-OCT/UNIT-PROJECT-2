MISSION 01 · 1985

STS-51-G

Saudi Arabia's first journey into space.

A Historic First

On June 17, 1985, Prince Sultan bin Salman became the first Saudi astronaut to travel into space. He flew aboard NASA's Space Shuttle Discovery as a payload specialist on the STS-51-G mission.

During the mission, the crew deployed communication satellites, including ARABSAT-1B, and conducted scientific activities in orbit.

MISSION 02 · 2023

AXIOM MISSION 2

A new generation of Saudi astronauts.

Saudi Arabia Reaches the ISS

On May 21, 2023, Saudi astronauts Rayyanah Barnawi and Ali Alqarni launched aboard Axiom Mission 2 to the International Space Station.

Rayyanah Barnawi became the first Saudi woman to travel to space. During the mission, the crew conducted scientific research and educational activities in microgravity.
