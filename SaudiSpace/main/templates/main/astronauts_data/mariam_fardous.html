A LIFE OF
DISCOVERY.

Mariam Fardous is a Saudi physician, scientist, and astronaut who joined the Saudi National Astronaut Program in 2022. Her interests in science, exploration, and adventure inspired her to pursue human spaceflight.

She has more than 13 years of experience in the medical field. Throughout her career, she worked in public health, data analysis, and epidemiology, using reporting tools and performance indicators to support management and strategic planning.

Fardous completed astronaut training and became qualified to participate in future space missions. She was selected as a backup mission specialist for Axiom Mission 2.

Raised in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, she hopes to inspire future generations to pursue their ambitions, explore new possibilities, and contribute to improving quality of life for humanity.
