Ali Alghamdi is a Saudi astronaut and pilot whose passion for aviation inspired him to pursue the field of human spaceflight. He joined the Saudi National Astronaut Program in 2022.

Alghamdi has more than 11 years of experience in jet aviation. During his aviation career, he participated in numerous airshows and gained extensive experience in flying and leading aircraft operations.

He completed the astronaut training program, qualifying him to participate in future human spaceflight missions. He was also selected as a backup crew member for Axiom Mission 2.

Growing up in Baljurashi, Saudi Arabia, Alghamdi developed a strong passion for flying. He hopes to encourage more young Saudis to explore science, aviation, and the possibilities of space exploration.
