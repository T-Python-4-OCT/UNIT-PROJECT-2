01 — HIS STORY
FROM THE SKIES TO SPACE

A JOURNEY
OF AMBITION.

Ali Alqarni is a Saudi astronaut and experienced fighter pilot who served as a mission specialist on Axiom Mission 2 (Ax-2), traveling to the International Space Station in 2023.

He has more than 12 years of flying experience and approximately 2,387 flight hours. His professional background includes operating F-15SA fighter aircraft, as well as experience with several other aircraft types.

Throughout his career, Alqarni developed experience in aircraft operations, team coordination, and managing complex mission scenarios. These skills supported his role in carrying out scientific activities in space.

His interest in space exploration grew after visiting NASA's Johnson Space Center during professional training in the United States. He later joined the Saudi National Astronaut Program and became part of the Ax-2 crew.

02 — SPACE MISSION

BEYOND
THE SKIES.

In May 2023, Ali Alqarni travelled to the International Space Station as a member of Axiom Mission 2 alongside Saudi astronaut Rayyanah Barnawi. During the mission, the crew conducted scientific research in microgravity.
