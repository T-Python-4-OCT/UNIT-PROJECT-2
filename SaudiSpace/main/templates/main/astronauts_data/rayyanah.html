A JOURNEY
OF DISCOVERY.

Rayyanah Barnawi is a Research Laboratory Specialist and the first Saudi woman to travel to space as a crew member of the SSA-HSF1 mission. She is recognized for her experience and contributions to scientific research.

Barnawi has more than nine years of experience in stem cell research and tissue re-engineering programs. Throughout her career, she has improved research protocols, troubleshot laboratory techniques, managed breast cancer research projects, and contributed to scientific publications in her field.

Her participation in the Saudi human spaceflight mission made history. She travelled alongside fellow Saudi astronaut Ali Alqarni, representing the Kingdom in a mission to the International Space Station.

Growing up in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, Barnawi developed a passion for experiments and scientific research. This passion motivated her to apply to the Saudi National Astronaut Program, with the ambition of conducting scientific research in a microgravity environment to serve her country and humanity.
