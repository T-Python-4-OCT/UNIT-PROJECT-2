01 / THE PURPOSE

SCIENCE THAT
GOES BEYOND
OUR PLANET.

Space provides a unique environment for scientific research. Microgravity allows researchers to study biological, physical, and material processes in ways that are difficult to reproduce on Earth.

Through its Human Space Flight Program, the Saudi Space Agency supports scientific experiments that can contribute to human health, technology, and future space exploration.

01 / BIOLOGY
CELL SCIENCE

Experiments in Cell Science

Researchers studied cellular responses in microgravity, including messenger RNA and inflammatory processes. This work can help improve understanding of biological changes during spaceflight.

02 / MEDICAL
HUMAN HEALTH

Blood-Based Biomarkers

This research investigates blood-based biomarkers to better understand the effects of spaceflight on the human brain and help assess astronaut health.

MEDICAL RESEARCH · SPACE HEALTH
03 / CLIMATE
WEATHER & CLIMATE

Cloud Seeding in Microgravity

Researchers compared cloud-seeding processes on Earth and in microgravity to understand how gravity influences water vapor and condensation.
